# Course rules that bind the project

Excerpts from the Canvas syllabus of XM_0059 (course 90260), fetched 2026-11-06.

## Practical assignment (syllabus)

> In groups of 3-4 people, you will work on a project throughout the period to develop your own
> knowledge-based system. You will come up with a project goal and topic yourself, and are
> required to implement a knowledge base using two of the formalisms presented in the lectures.
> You will describe your system in a report, where you will discuss the system, illustrate
> reasoning processes and compare the experiences with the two used knowledge representation
> formalism. The project will be peer-reviewed in two phases: an initial report needs to be
> submitted in the middle of period 2, and a final version will be submitted at the end of the
> period. Each student is required to review two initial versions and two final versions of the
> report. The project makes 35% of the final grade, and again a grade of at least 5.5 is
> required to pass the course.

## Use of generative AI (syllabus)

> You are not allowed to use generative AI such as Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT to create any
> portion of the text in the project report - if we suspect that you did otherwise, we will have
> to report this to the examination board and it may lead to the failing of the course. You may
> however use generative AI to help you write scripts or code that support you in your project.
> If you have a cool and creative idea in which using an LLM could be used as part of your
> project, please contact me and I can let you know whether this is okay. In any case, note down
> any AI use and mention it in your report, to avoid misunderstandings - failing to do so may
> lead to your project being flagged as fraud!
>
> To verify that your project is indeed yours, we would like to see a version history of your
> report, your knowledge base and any supporting implementations. The easiest way to do this is
> by using a github project, and/or an overleaf project for your latex code of the project.

From lecture 01 (2026-10-27): using GenAI to get feedback on text you wrote yourself is allowed.
The report is a 10-page LaTeX document.
